ShizuStore is a catalogue of Android apps that make the most of Shizuku.

Shizuku lets apps use system APIs without root, but finding apps that support it means digging through lists and forums. ShizuStore gathers them from the awesome-shizuku project into one searchable catalogue, with icons, descriptions, screenshots, signatures and download links.

Features:

* A curated catalogue. Listings come from the awesome-shizuku list, so every app shown is one that uses Shizuku.

* Direct downloads. When an app publishes APKs, ShizuStore downloads the file from the upstream server, never from a mirror, and verifies it against the published SHA-256 checksum before installing.

* Four install methods. Session, system installer, root, and Shizuku. Root and Shizuku install without a confirmation dialog for each app.

* Signature checks. The installed certificate is compared against the fingerprints published for each app, and a version signed with a different key is never offered as an update.

* Links when downloads are not available. Apps that only ship on Google Play open their Play listing, and apps without a public APK open their website.

* Updates. Check for new versions on an interval of your choosing, and download and install them automatically where silent installs are available.

* Favourites, blacklist and ignored updates. Mark the apps you follow, hide packages you never want to see, and skip a specific version of an individual app.

* No tracking. No analytics, no accounts, no third-party services. The catalogue server is public and unauthenticated.

* Free software. GPLv3, developed in the open.

ShizuStore is not affiliated with the Shizuku project or the awesome-shizuku list.
